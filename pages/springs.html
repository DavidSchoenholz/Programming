Springs, not curves.

Every animation on this page is the same twenty lines of physics: a mass, a spring pulling it home, and some friction. Change the numbers and the personality changes.

1. Pull and let go

Drag the ball anywhere. The trace underneath shows its distance from home over time.

2. Boop

Hover (or tap) an icon. It gets nudged, then the spring brings it back.

3. Follow the leader

Each dot springs towards the one in front. Move your pointer inside the box.

4. A switch with some give

Same spring, low damping. It overshoots a little, like a real thing would.
